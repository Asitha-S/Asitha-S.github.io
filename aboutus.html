Our Story

Connecting Pets with Loving Homes Since 2024

Our Mission

At FurBound, we believe every pet deserves a loving home. Our mission is to connect abandoned, surrendered, and rescued animals with caring families, while educating the community about responsible pet ownership.

How We Started

FurBound began when our founder volunteered at a local animal shelter and saw firsthand the number of wonderful pets waiting for homes. What started as a small foster network in 2024 has grown into a community of passionate volunteers, foster families, and supporters dedicated to animal welfare.

Today, we work with shelters across the region to find homes for hundreds of dogs, cats, and other pets each year.

Our Values

  • Compassion: We treat every animal with kindness and dignity.
  • Education: We promote responsible pet ownership and care.
  • Community: We build connections between pets, adopters, and animal lovers.
  • Transparency: We are open about our processes and how we use donations.

Join Our Mission

We're always looking for passionate volunteers and foster families. If you'd like to help, please contact us directly.
